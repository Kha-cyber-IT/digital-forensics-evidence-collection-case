DIGITAL FORENSICS WORKFLOW - TRAINING NOTES

1. Secure scene and control access.
2. Document surroundings before touching devices.
3. Identify all digital evidence sources.
4. Record current power and network states.
5. Prioritize volatile evidence, especially live RAM.
6. Photograph active displays, notifications and indicators.
7. Make device-specific preservation decisions.
8. Document collection, packaging and seals.
9. Record every custody handoff.
10. Acquire forensic images using approved procedures.
11. Calculate and verify cryptographic hashes.
12. Analyze working copies rather than original evidence.
13. Maintain examiner notes and produce a factual report.

WARNING
This case demonstrates initial identification and preservation.
No actual forensic acquisition or analysis has been performed.
